One favour, before you go

Please do this bit at home. We will be so grateful.

Download its brain on wifi

3.11 GB, one time only, and then it's yours forever. It won't touch your data plan, and out there in the dust you'll have nothing to download it with anyway. The Gerlach parking lot is a heartbreaking place to discover this.

iPhone only for now, sorry. A newer one gets the full brain. An older one gets a smaller brain, and says so. A really old one tells you straight away that it can't, rather than let you find out after twenty minutes.

It'll get things wrong

Of course it will. There's no tech support and nobody on call, so you'll figure it out like we figure out everything else. When it says something daft, shout about it in the thread.

And trust people over the app, always. Please don't dig a hole at 2am because an app told you to.
