## **Myths, Pranks, and Running Jokes**

### **Daft Punk at the Trash Fence**

The most enduring prank in Burning Man history.

* **The Myth:** Every year, a rumor circulates that the electronic duo **Daft Punk** will play a secret set at the "Trash Fence" (Deep Playa) usually at 4:00 AM or sunrise.  
* **The Reality:** They have never played there. It is a hazing ritual for new attendees ("Burgins") to send them cycling miles out into the dark for nothing.  
* **Cultural Significance:** It tests gullibility and often leads to spontaneous parties at the fence among those who were fooled.

### **"It Was Better Next Year"**

* **Meaning:** A satirical twist on "It was better last year."  
* **Usage:** Used to mock the constant veteran complaint that the event has "jumped the shark" or "sold out." It acknowledges that the best burn is always the one you haven't had yet (or the one you are currently planning).

### **"Safety Third"**

* **Origin:** Derived from the **Cacophony Society** (one of the event's parent groups) and the "Danger Ranger" ethos.  
* **Meaning:** It does not mean safety is unimportant. It means that **Fun** and **Style** (or Art/Experience) come first and second. It is a rejection of the overly litigious, padded-corner safety culture of the Default World.  
* **Context:** Used when doing something slightly dangerous but exhilarating (e.g., climbing an art structure, riding a flaming skee-ball machine).

### **The Golden Spike**

* **Definition:** The exact geographic survey point from which the entire city grid is measured.  
* **Ceremony:** Driven into the ground months before the event by the survey team. It is the "Axis Mundi" of Black Rock City. When the event ends, the spike is removed, and the city theoretically disappears.

## **Core Rituals and Traditions**

### **The Temple vs. The Man**

* **The Man Burn (Saturday):** A raucous, chaotic, high-energy celebration. Involves screaming, fireworks, shouting, and explosions. Represents the burning of "the system" or "the self."  
* **The Temple Burn (Sunday):** A solemn, silent event.  
  * **The Ritual:** Participants write messages to lost loved ones, grief, or past traumas on the Temple walls throughout the week.  
  * **The Atmosphere:** Tens of thousands of people sit in absolute silence as the structure burns. It is a mass funeral/catharsis.  
  * **Etiquette:** **Do not scream** "Burn it\!" or cheer during the Temple burn. It is considered deeply disrespectful.

### **The Sunset Howl**

* **Action:** As the sun dips below the mountains, a wave of howling (like wolves) spreads across the city.  
* **Meaning:** Acknowledging the transition from day (hot, bright, lethargic) to night (cool, neon, energetic).

### **The Lamplighters**

* **Who they are:** A specific volunteer group dressed in monk-like robes.  
* **The Ritual:** Every evening near sunset, they process through the city carrying kerosene lanterns on long poles to light the spires along the Esplanade and the Keyhole.  
* **Significance:** One of the few "old world" rituals that predates modern LED technology. They force the city to slow down for a moment of ceremony.

### **"Welcome Home"**

* **The Greeters:** Upon passing the Gate, you encounter Greeters.  
* **The Ritual:** They will ask if you are a virgin. If yes, you are asked to get out, roll in the dust ("Dust Angel"), and ring a bell.  
* **The Phrase:** "Welcome Home" is the standard greeting, implying that BRC is your *true* home and the rest of the world is just a temporary visit.

## **Social Dynamics and Etiquette**

### **Playa Names**

* **The Rule:** You generally do not choose your own Playa Name; it is given to you by others based on something you did, a personality quirk, or a mishap.  
* **Acceptance:** You can refuse a name if you hate it, but if three independent people call you by it, it sticks.  
* **Purpose:** Shedding your Default World identity (doctor, lawyer, coder) to be judged solely on your immediate actions and interactions.

### **Consent and Photography**

* **The Red Zone:** Photography is increasingly controversial.  
* **Rule:** Ask before taking a portrait.  
* **Porn/Nudity:** BRC allows public nudity. However, taking photos of naked people without explicit, enthusiastic consent is a serious violation.  
* **Influencers:** "Plug and Play" camps or influencers staging photo shoots with product placement are actively hunted by the community and shamed (see: "Decommodification").

### **Shirtcocking**

* **Definition:** The act of wearing a t-shirt but no pants or underwear.  
* **Stereotype:** Predominantly associated with older males.  
* **Cultural Reception:** Often the butt of jokes (pun intended). Many camps have "No Shirtcocking" signs at their bars.

### **The "Darkwad"**

* **Definition:** A person moving through the playa at night with no lights.  
* **Danger:** They are invisible to art cars and cyclists.  
* **Response:** It is socially acceptable to yell "Darkwad\!" at them to warn them of their invisibility.

## **Art and Mutant Vehicles**

### **The DMV (Department of Mutant Vehicles)**

* **Function:** They are the gatekeepers of art cars.  
* **Mutation Standard:** To get a license, a vehicle must be "radically mutated." You cannot just glue some fur to a golf cart. The base vehicle should not be recognizable.  
* **Day vs. Night Licenses:**  
  * **Day License:** Can drive during the day.  
  * **Night License:** Requires "radically illuminated" status. Must be bright enough to be seen from any angle.

### **Famous Sound Cars (The "Big Dogs")**

* **Robot Heart:** A legendary bus known for deep house/techno and sunrise sets in deep playa.  
* **Mayan Warrior:** A high-tech art car featuring Aztec/Mexican futurist aesthetics and lasers (Note: The original burned down in 2023; a new one was built for 2024).  
* **Role:** These cars act as mobile nightclubs. People "chase" them on bikes into the deep playa.

### **Art Support Camps**

* **Definition:** Camps that exist solely to build and maintain a massive art piece (e.g., The Temple crew).  
* **Culture:** Hard labor, early mornings, high stress. Very different from "party camps."

## **History and Origins (Deep Context)**

### **Baker Beach (The Origin)**

* **Date:** 1986\.  
* **Event:** Larry Harvey and Jerry James burned a wooden man effigy on a San Francisco beach with a handful of people.  
* **Evolution:** It moved to the Black Rock Desert in 1990 after being shut down by Park Police.

### **The Cacophony Society**

* **Definition:** A San Francisco-based group of pranksters and urban explorers dedicated to "experiences beyond the pale."  
* **Influence:** They brought the concept of "Zone Trips" (surreal excursions). Burning Man was originally "Zone Trip \#4."  
* **Legacy:** They injected the chaos, pranks, and costuming culture into what was originally just a bonfire.

### **The Drive-By Shooting Range**

* **History:** In the early 90s (the "Anarchy Days"), you could drive cars on the playa while shooting guns.  
* **The Shift:** In 1996/1997, the event grew too big. A participant was run over in a tent, and another was injured.  
* **Result:** The "grid" was established, driving was banned (except for art cars), and guns were banned. This marked the transition from "Anarchy" to "Civic Responsibility."

### **Moop Public Enemy \#1: Feathers**

* **Rule:** Feathers (boas, headdresses) are essentially banned.  
* **Why:** They detach easily in the wind, are impossible to pick up (MOOP), and embed in the desert floor.  
* **Reaction:** If you wear a feather boa, you will likely be heckled and asked to put it away. Glitter is Public Enemy \#2.
